# Drone Roof Inspection

Service by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services/drone-roof-inspection

High-resolution aerial imagery of the whole roof, including steep sections, skylights, and edges, without an inspector walking the surface.

- Flights follow FAA Part 107. AVID obtains authorizations first near airports or in controlled airspace.
- Thermal imaging is added where warranted. Drone imagery alone does not detect subsurface moisture, so AVID adds infrared scans or nuclear moisture surveys when moisture is suspected.
- Deliverables: a written condition report, numbered and photographed deficiencies ranked by severity, remaining useful life estimates, repair recommendations, replacement cost projections, and a permanent photo record for insurance, warranty, and capital planning.
- Available as a standalone inspection (capital planning, acquisition due diligence, warranty documentation) or as part of the biannual [Roof Asset Management Program](roof-asset-management.md), with the same method and report format across a portfolio.
