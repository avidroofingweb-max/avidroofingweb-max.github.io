# Waterproofing & Restoration

Service by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services/waterproofing-restoration

Finding where water actually enters a building and sealing the envelope: walls, decks, joints, and below-grade systems. Restoration of aging surfaces is offered as an alternative to tear-out where the substrate is sound.

## Surfaces
Tilt-wall and expansion joints, window perimeters, walkways, staircases, balconies, plaza decks, parking structures, stairwells, mechanical rooms, facades, dock and loading areas, and foundation and below-grade walls.

## Methods
Non-invasive leak detection, moisture testing, infrared imaging, and controlled water testing to find the true source first. Systems include fluid-applied and sheet-applied waterproofing, coatings, sealants, and injection grouting.

## Process
Diagnose the source, deliver a written report of cause and fix, restore the surfaces, and document the work for warranty and capital planning. The same team diagnoses and completes the work, phased so occupied buildings keep running.

## Lifespan
Properly installed systems last 10 to 25 years depending on type, substrate, and traffic. Traffic-bearing systems sit at the low end.

Roof coatings restore existing roof membranes; waterproofing membranes protect non-roof surfaces. See [Waterproofing](waterproofing.md) and [Roof Coating](roof-coating.md).
