# Skylight Installation

Service by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services/skylight-installation

Fixed and operable commercial skylights, tubular daylighting devices, smoke vents, and custom skylight systems on flat and low-slope roofs, including curb framing, flashing, and sealing to manufacturer specs. Glazing is acrylic or polycarbonate.

- When a certified installer uses certified flashing, the surrounding membrane warranty stays intact.
- Commercial skylights last 20 to 30 years; flashing usually fails before the unit does.
- Sealant should be checked yearly. Reflashing works if the frame and glazing are sound; yellowed or cracked glazing means replacement. Acrylic yellows faster than polycarbonate.
- Cost depends on size, material, roof type, and opening complexity. Retrofits cost more than new construction.
