# Waterproofing

Service by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services/waterproofing

Waterproofing for the surfaces where the roof ends: parking decks, facade walls and masonry, foundation walls, balconies, terraces, plaza decks, bridge decks, and loading docks.

## Systems
Liquid-applied and sheet-applied membranes, traffic-bearing deck coatings, below-grade waterproofing, joint sealants, and restoration systems, chosen for substrate, water exposure, movement, traffic, and grade.

## Warning signs
Stains after rain, efflorescence, growing cracks, spalling concrete, rust stains, mold or mildew, peeling facade coatings, and ponding on decks or terraces.

## Lifespan
Liquid-applied membranes typically last 10 to 15 years; sheet membranes can last 20+. High-traffic areas wear faster. AVID gives the expected service life and maintenance needs for each system it recommends.

Work on occupied buildings is coordinated with a project manager and a building-specific plan. Pricing is project-specific and depends on area, substrate, complexity, access, and surface condition.
