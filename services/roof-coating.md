# Roof Coating

Service by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services/roof-coating

Silicone and acrylic liquid-applied coatings for sound commercial roofs. A coating restores waterproofing, improves reflectivity, and extends service life without a tear-off.

## Is the roof a candidate?
- Good candidate: sound structure with an aging membrane or minor leaks, including mid-life roofs with a few isolated wet spots.
- Replace instead: widespread membrane deterioration or leaks, compromised structure, or a roof near the end of its life.
- AVID will decline to coat a roof that isn't a candidate. Coating a failing roof is an expensive delay.

## Silicone vs. acrylic
- Silicone: more durable and moisture-resistant, handles ponding water.
- Acrylic: more cost-effective and more reflective, suited to energy-savings goals.

## Prep decides the outcome
Adhesion testing, pressure washing, seam and joint reinforcement, patching, and confirming the surface is completely dry before application. Dirt, algae, rust, and trapped moisture cause coating failures.

## Numbers
- Typically adds 10 to 15 years of life on a sound substrate
- Typically 20 to 30% of the cost of a full reroof
- Some silicone systems carry 10-year manufacturer warranties, which require certified installation
- Most jobs finish in one day; multi-building scopes take several days
- Reflective coatings lower rooftop temperatures and cooling load

Substrates: TPO, PVC, EPDM, modified bitumen, BUR, metal, and spray polyurethane foam, each with a matched coating. Example project: [The Good Shepherd, Longview](../proof/projects.md).
