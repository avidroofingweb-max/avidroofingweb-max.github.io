# Hail Damage Roof Repair

Service by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services/hail-damage-roof-repair

Hail damage assessments done by roofers, not adjusters.

- After a storm AVID inspects the roof, using drone and thermal imaging where useful, and documents damage with drone imagery, close-ups of each impact, core data where applicable, a written assessment, and a priced repair or replacement scope.
- AVID coordinates with the carrier and adjuster but does not file the claim, interpret coverage, or act as a public adjuster.
- Owners should notify the carrier promptly, check the policy's notice requirements, and document the roof before permanent repairs.
- Warning signs: dents, punctures, cracks, granule loss, and leaks after a hail event. Isolated damage may be repairable; widespread damage usually points to replacement.
- For active leaks, you will hear from AVID within 24 hours while permanent repairs are evaluated.

Roof warranties cover defects and workmanship, not weather. Storm damage is a property insurance matter.
