# Roof Replacement

Service by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services/roof-replacement

AVID recommends replacement only when a roof's condition warrants it, and inspects (including core cuts on flat roofs) before recommending one.

## When replacement makes sense
Recurring leaks in several areas, widespread wet insulation in core cuts, a membrane over 20 years old with no maintenance history, large areas of blistering or splitting, or inadequate drainage. A single leak at one seam or penetration points to repair.

## Tear-off vs. recover
A recover (new membrane over the existing roof) works only when the existing roof is dry, sound, and a single layer. Wet insulation, a compromised deck, or two existing layers require tear-off.

## System choice
TPO is the most common low-slope choice. PVC suits grease or chemical exhaust (restaurants, food processing). Modified bitumen suits high-traffic roofs. Metal is for maximum longevity.

## Cost and timeline
- $7 to $15 per sq ft installed in DFW
- A 20,000 sq ft reroof typically takes one to three weeks depending on system, weather, crew size, and occupancy
- A written, fixed-price scope and a production schedule come before crews mobilize
