# Commercial Roofing

Service by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services/commercial-roofing

Commercial roofing systems planned around the property, tenants, and budget, from inspection and specification through installation, warranty, and ongoing service. One accountable partner for the whole roof lifecycle.

## Systems
TPO, PVC, EPDM, modified bitumen, built-up roofing (BUR), metal panel systems, and hybrid assemblies, specified for each building.

## Process
1. Inspection and documented condition assessment
2. Scope of repair, restoration, coating, or replacement, with the reasoning for each option
3. Installation led by experienced field leaders
4. Documentation of conditions, scope, and progress for owners, insurers, and finance teams
5. Post-completion support and warranty

## Cost and timeline
- Typically $7 to $15 per sq ft installed in DFW. TPO and modified bitumen sit at the low end; PVC, fully adhered systems, and metal run higher. Accurate pricing needs an inspection.
- About 6 to 10 weeks from signed contract to substantial completion for a typical 50,000 to 100,000 sq ft single-ply reroof on an occupied building. A phase-by-phase schedule comes with the proposal.

## Warranty
Manufacturer warranties of 10 to 30 years plus a separate workmanship warranty, both in writing before work starts. See [Certifications and suppliers](../business/certifications-and-suppliers.md).
