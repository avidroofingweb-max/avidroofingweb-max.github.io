# AVID services

Fourteen commercial services. Overview page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services

- [24/7 Emergency Roof Repair](emergency-roof-repair.md) — Emergency response to active leaks and storm damage on commercial roofs: stabilization, documentation, and a permanent repair plan.
- [Commercial Roofing](commercial-roofing.md) — Full-lifecycle commercial roofing in DFW: TPO, PVC, EPDM, modified bitumen, BUR, metal, and hybrid systems on occupied buildings.
- [Design-Build Reroof](design-build-reroof.md) — One contract for assessment, design, submittals, and installation, with design and build overlapping to compress the schedule.
- [Roof Asset Management](roof-asset-management.md) — Roof Asset Management Program (RAMP): biannual inspections, documented baselines, and multi-year capital plans for single buildings and portfolios.
- [Architectural Sheet Metal](architectural-sheet-metal.md) — Shop-fabricated copings, fascia, edge metal, gutters, and flashings detailed to the membrane and manufacturer specs.
- [Roof Coating](roof-coating.md) — Silicone and acrylic coating systems that add 10 to 15 years to sound commercial roofs at roughly 20 to 30% of replacement cost.
- [Waterproofing & Restoration](waterproofing-restoration.md) — Finding where water enters a building and sealing the envelope: tilt-wall and expansion joints, decks, parking structures, facades, below-grade walls.
- [Waterproofing](waterproofing.md) — Commercial waterproofing beyond the roof: parking decks, facades, foundations, balconies, plaza decks, and loading docks.
- [Roof Repair](roof-repair.md) — Commercial roof repair that finds the real cause: seams, flashings, penetrations, drains, and parapets.
- [Roof Replacement](roof-replacement.md) — Commercial roof replacement in DFW, recommended only when the roof warrants it: tear-off vs. recover, system choice, cost, and timeline.
- [Roof Maintenance](roof-maintenance.md) — Scheduled commercial roof maintenance: biannual inspections, drain clearing, minor repairs, and written reports that keep warranties valid.
- [Hail Damage Roof Repair](hail-damage-roof-repair.md) — Commercial hail damage assessments by roofers: drone and thermal documentation and coordination with the carrier and adjuster.
- [Drone Roof Inspection](drone-roof-inspection.md) — FAA Part 107 drone roof inspections with optional thermal imaging, numbered deficiency callouts, and remaining-life estimates.
- [Skylight Installation](skylight-installation.md) — Commercial skylight installation and reflashing on flat and low-slope roofs: fixed and operable units, tubular daylighting, smoke vents.
