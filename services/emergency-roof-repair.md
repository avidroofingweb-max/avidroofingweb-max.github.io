# 24/7 Emergency Roof Repair

Service by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services/emergency-roof-repair

Emergency response for active leaks and storm damage on commercial roofs: rapid stabilization, a documented damage assessment, and a recommended permanent repair.

- Call Emergency Dispatch at 1-833-766-3003, staffed 24/7 including nights, weekends, and holidays.
- AVID responds to every emergency call within 24 hours. Properties under a Roof Asset Management Triage Agreement get temporary dry-in within 72 hours.
- Stabilization: tarping, membrane patches, stopping active water entry, and temporary dry-in once conditions are safe.
- Documentation for insurance: photos, a record of the stabilization work, and a repair or replacement scope for the adjuster. AVID does not act as a public adjuster. Emergency work may be claimable depending on the policy, so notify the carrier promptly.
- One point of contact through the event, coordinated around tenants and operations.
