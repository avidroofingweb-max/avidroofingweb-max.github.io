# Roof Maintenance

Service by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services/roof-maintenance

Scheduled maintenance programs tailored to each roof's system, age, and exposure.

- Inspections at least twice a year (spring and fall) and after major storms, set through the [Roof Asset Management Program](roof-asset-management.md).
- Each visit covers seams, flashings, ponding, blocked drains, and surface wear; clears drains, scuppers, and debris; and makes minor repairs.
- A written inspection report after every visit.
- Most manufacturer warranties (TPO, PVC, EPDM, modified bitumen) require documented biannual inspections to stay valid.
- Pricing depends on roof condition and square footage.
