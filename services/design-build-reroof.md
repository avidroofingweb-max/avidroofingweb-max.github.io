# Design-Build Reroof

Service by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services/design-build-reroof

One AVID team handles assessment, design, submittals, and installation under one contract, with one accountable project manager. Design and construction overlap instead of running in sequence, and the bid-and-rebid cycle goes away.

## Benefits
Faster delivery, single-source accountability, tighter cost control, no separate consultant fee on top of the install, and one warranty covering the whole assembly. Change orders are rare.

## Best fit
Complex roofs with multiple sections, mixed systems, or many penetrations, and large projects that need schedule compression or budget certainty.

## Process
1. Assess actual conditions: membrane, insulation R-value, deck, drainage, and penetrations
2. Design the system (membrane, insulation, detailing) for that building
3. Make value-engineering decisions together during design
4. Produce drawings, specifications, schedule, and budget
5. Sequence design, procurement, and installation so the building stays operational
6. Install with warranty requirements built in from the start

Work on occupied hotels, medical facilities, and retail centers is phased by zone, with noise and odor controlled and egress kept clear. Manufacturer warranties run 10 to 30 years depending on the system.
