# Roof Asset Management

Service by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services/roof-asset-management

The Roof Asset Management Program (RAMP) is a systematic way to track, maintain, and budget for a commercial roof portfolio over time. It suits owners, property managers, facility directors, and organizations with multiple properties, including schools, healthcare campuses, retail centers, industrial facilities, and national portfolios.

## What's included
- A documented baseline condition assessment for each roof: age, system, known repairs, moisture surveys where warranted, and remaining life
- Biannual GPS-tagged inspections (spring and fall) plus inspections after significant storms
- Drone imagery, core sampling where warranted, moisture survey data, and photo documentation
- Remaining useful life estimates, prioritized repair schedules, and annual capital replacement budgets in today's dollars, adjusted for DFW market conditions
- Condition reports for ownership, finance, or lenders, and portfolio-level comparison across buildings
- Optional emergency response and warranty tracking

## Triage Agreement
Properties covered by a Roof Asset Management Triage Agreement get temporary dry-in within 72 hours after an emergency call. See [Emergency response](../business/emergency-response.md).

## Why inspect regularly
Early detection at seams, flashings, and penetrations heads off deck damage. The page's example: a $400 repair versus a $15,000 to $50,000 deck replacement. Most manufacturer warranties also require documented biannual inspections.
