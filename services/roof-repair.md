# Roof Repair

Service by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services/roof-repair

Most leaks don't need a new roof. AVID repairs TPO, PVC, EPDM, modified bitumen, and metal roofs, starting with an inspection to find the underlying cause.

- Common leak sources: failed seams (the most common), flashing at HVAC units, pipes, and curbs, punctures, clogged drains or scuppers, and separated counterflashing at parapets.
- New membrane sections are installed to manufacturer spec so existing manufacturer warranties aren't voided. Workmanship warranty terms come in writing before work begins.
- Written estimates are based on inspection findings. Replacement is worth considering when repair costs approach 40% of replacement.
- Ignored leaks lead to wet insulation, rot, corrosion, mold, and electrical hazards.
- For an active leak, call Emergency Dispatch and you will hear from AVID within 24 hours. See [Emergency response](../business/emergency-response.md).
