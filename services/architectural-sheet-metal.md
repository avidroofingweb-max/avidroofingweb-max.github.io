# Architectural Sheet Metal

Service by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/services/architectural-sheet-metal

Custom copings, fascia, edge metal, and flashings fabricated in AVID's own shop, plus gutters, downspouts, gravel stops, scupper boxes, collector heads, expansion joint covers, and through-wall and custom penetration flashings.

- Designed to the membrane being installed (TPO, PVC, modified bitumen, EPDM, or metal panel) and detailed to manufacturer specs to keep warranties intact.
- Typically 24-gauge steel, aluminum, or copper, with gauges and finishes chosen for the North Texas climate and wind-uplift requirements.
- Edges matter because most commercial roof failures start there, where wind uplift concentrates.
- Properly fabricated commercial sheet metal typically lasts 30 to 50 years. Failures usually come from fastening, laps, or sealants, not the metal.
- Sheet metal can be repaired on its own (failing copings, separated gutters, rusted scuppers) without a reroof when the membrane is sound.
- The same firm fabricates, installs, and warrants the work.
