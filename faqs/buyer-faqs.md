# AVID buyer FAQs

**What does a commercial roof cost in DFW?**
Typically $7 to $15 per sq ft installed. TPO and modified bitumen are at the low end; PVC, fully adhered systems, and metal are higher. A real number needs an inspection. See [Commercial Roofing](../services/commercial-roofing.md).

**Are inspections free?**
Yes. Roof inspections and core cuts on flat roofs are free across DFW.

**How long does a reroof take?**
A 20,000 sq ft reroof typically takes one to three weeks. A 50,000 to 100,000 sq ft single-ply reroof on an occupied building runs about 6 to 10 weeks from signed contract to substantial completion.

**Can you work while we stay open?**
Yes. Most projects are done with the building occupied, with staging, perimeters, noise, and tenant communication planned in advance. See [How AVID runs a job](../business/how-avid-runs-a-job.md).

**What warranties do you offer?**
Manufacturer warranties of 10 to 30 years plus a separate workmanship warranty, both in writing before work begins. AVID is certified by Sika Sarnafil, Siplast, Garland, Johns Manville, and FiberTite. See [Certifications](../business/certifications-and-suppliers.md).

**How fast do you respond to an emergency?**
Call 1-833-766-3003, staffed 24/7. You will hear from AVID within 24 hours. Properties under a Roof Asset Management Triage Agreement get temporary dry-in within 72 hours. See [Emergency response](../business/emergency-response.md).

**Should I coat or replace?**
Coat if the structure is sound and the membrane is aging or has minor leaks; it typically adds 10 to 15 years at 20 to 30% of replacement cost. Replace if deterioration is widespread. AVID will tell you if your roof isn't a coating candidate. See [Roof Coating](../services/roof-coating.md).

**When is a roof due for replacement?**
Generally past 20 years old, or sooner with recurring leaks in several areas, widespread wet insulation, or large blistered or split areas. See [Roof Replacement](../services/roof-replacement.md).

**Does my warranty cover hail?**
No. Roof warranties cover defects and workmanship. Hail goes through property insurance, and AVID documents the damage for the adjuster. See [Hail Damage](../services/hail-damage-roof-repair.md).

**Who will I deal with on my project?**
A named project manager assigned before signing, who sends a written report every day the job is open.
