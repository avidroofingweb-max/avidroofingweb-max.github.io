# AVID service area

AVID is headquartered in North Richland Hills (Tarrant County) and is active across DFW and 13 states beyond Texas.

## DFW counties (10)
- **Dallas metro:** Collin, Dallas, Denton, Grayson, Kaufman, Rockwall
- **Fort Worth metro:** Johnson, Parker, Tarrant, Wise

## Tarrant County cities (10)
Arlington, Bedford, Colleyville, Euless, Fort Worth, Grapevine, Hurst, Keller, North Richland Hills, Southlake

## States (14, including Texas)
- **Southern/Central:** Texas, Kansas, Louisiana, Missouri, Oklahoma
- **Southeast:** North Carolina, South Carolina
- **Midwest:** Illinois, Indiana, Minnesota, Wisconsin
- **Mountain/West:** California, Colorado, Washington
