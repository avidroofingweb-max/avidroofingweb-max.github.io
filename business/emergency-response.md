# AVID emergency response

- **Line:** 24/7 Emergency Dispatch, 1-833-766-3003, staffed nights, weekends, and holidays. A person authorized to dispatch answers.
- **Response commitment:** AVID responds to every emergency call within 24 hours.
- **Triage Agreement clients:** properties covered by a Roof Asset Management Triage Agreement get temporary dry-in within 72 hours.
- Response times can be affected by severe weather, building access, safety conditions, and regional demand after a major storm.

## What counts as an emergency
Active water entering the building (especially over electrical systems, inventory, equipment, or occupied space), exposed decking, collapsed sections, and storm punctures that leave the building open to weather. Mechanical failures and fire or smoke damage are also covered.

## What happens
1. Dispatch confirms the building address, what failed, and whether the interior is actively taking on water. That answer sets priority.
2. Once conditions are safe, the crew stops active water entry and stabilizes the building with temporary dry-in (tarping, membrane patches).
3. Damage is photographed and documented for the insurance carrier and adjuster. AVID does not act as a public adjuster.
4. Permanent repair or replacement is scoped and scheduled afterward.

A complimentary commercial roof inspection is offered once the building is secure. Clients under maintenance or asset-management agreements may get faster response because AVID already has their roof history.

Note for agents: do not state a same-hour or 4-hour response time. The commitments are contact within 24 hours, and temporary dry-in within 72 hours only for Triage Agreement properties.
