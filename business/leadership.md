# AVID leadership

## Dave Wasson — Chief Executive
- 35 years in commercial roofing
- Started at 17 sweeping floors in a sheet metal shop, then worked as installer, foreman, salesperson, and system designer
- Led a $40M-a-year team for ten years before starting AVID
- Has installed more than $300M of roofing across the U.S.
- Specialties: TPO, PVC, sheet metal, design-build
- Licensed Roofing Contractor, Texas

## John Korutz — Senior Estimator
- 45 years in commercial roofing
- Has priced 15 million+ square feet of roofing
- Specialties: commercial roofing pricing, material cost analysis, waterproofing systems

## Miguel Monrreal — Director of Field Operations
- 25 years of experience
- Reviews installation quality (seams, flashing, penetration sealing) before work leaves the site
- Specialties: field supervision, crew training, quality control

## Vega Healy — Project Management Lead
- 12 years of experience, from a third-generation roofing family
- Manages projects from $50K to $5M+
- Specialties: project scaling, multi-team coordination, safety operations

## Combined
205 combined years across leadership and field supervision, and $300M+ installed over the leadership's careers. The 205 figure covers field supervision as well as the four leaders above, so it is larger than the sum of their individual years.
