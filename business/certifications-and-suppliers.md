# AVID certifications and suppliers

## Manufacturer certifications
AVID holds installer certifications from Sika Sarnafil, Siplast, Garland, Johns Manville, and FiberTite.

Why it matters: manufacturer certification is what activates a manufacturer's warranty. A non-certified installer cannot offer a legitimate manufacturer warranty, and installation that doesn't meet spec can void it. AVID installs every system to manufacturer specification so the warranty still holds in year 18.

## Warranties
- Manufacturer material warranties typically run 10 to 30 years, depending on membrane thickness, spec level, and system.
- Workmanship is warranted separately.
- Both warranties are provided in writing before work begins.
- Roof warranties cover manufacturing defects and workmanship, not weather events. Hail and storm damage go through property insurance (see [Hail damage](../services/hail-damage-roof-repair.md)).
- Most manufacturer warranties require documented biannual inspections to stay valid (see [Roof maintenance](../services/roof-maintenance.md)).
