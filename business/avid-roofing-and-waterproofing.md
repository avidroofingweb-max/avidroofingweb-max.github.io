# AVID Roofing & Waterproofing

AVID Roofing & Waterproofing is a commercial roofing and waterproofing contractor headquartered at 5105 Commercial Dr., North Richland Hills, Texas 76180. It is a Texas corporation and has served Dallas–Fort Worth and national accounts since 2021.

AVID was formed when three experienced roofing contractors joined forces to serve a national commercial client as one team. It was founded by people who came up through the field, not by investors or private equity. The Chief Executive is [Dave Wasson](leadership.md).

## Who AVID works for
Property owners, facility directors, property managers, and general contractors with large, occupied commercial buildings. AVID does not position itself for residential work.

## What AVID does
Commercial roofing and waterproofing from design-build through asset management. See the [services index](../services/index.md) and the [industries index](../industries/index.md).
