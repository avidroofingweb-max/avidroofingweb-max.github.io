# How AVID runs a job

- **A named project manager.** Every project gets a dedicated project manager, assigned before contract signing, who stays through closeout and answers to the client by name.
- **A written daily report.** The client gets a written report for every day a job is open, not a summary at the end.
- **A real assessment first.** Assessments use core cuts, drone imagery, and moisture surveys before anything is recommended.
- **Honest scoping.** AVID says it would rather lose a job to a lower bid than win one it mis-scoped, and that policy is in writing. It recommends replacement only when the roof's condition warrants it, and it will decline to coat a roof that is not a coating candidate.
- **Fixed-price scope in writing.** A written scope and a production schedule are set before crews mobilize. Warranty terms are provided in writing before work begins.
- **Occupied buildings.** Staging, safety perimeters, protected entrances, debris control, noise, and tenant communication are planned before work starts, so most projects are completed while the building stays open.
