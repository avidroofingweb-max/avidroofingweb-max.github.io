# AVID safety program

- Every crew works to OSHA standards.
- Crews get regular safety training on accident and fall prevention, plus task-specific instructions before work starts.
- Job sites are inspected and audited by internal and third-party inspectors. Daily inspections and equipment checks are part of site protocol.
- Fall protection and PPE include harnesses, warning lines, guardrails, safety monitors, hard hats, gloves, eye protection, and high-visibility clothing, set by roof conditions, scope, access, and the hazards found in the pre-job assessment.
- A building-specific safety plan is written before work starts. Before mobilization the team reviews scope, roof access, fall hazards, occupied areas, equipment locations, material staging, and emergency procedures.
- Ground safety: cones around vehicles and work areas, cleared parking near work zones, protected entrances, debris containment, and marked perimeters.
- Crews monitor wind, rain, lightning, temperature, and roof-surface conditions, and stop work when risk is unacceptable. Materials and exposed areas are secured before crews leave the roof.
