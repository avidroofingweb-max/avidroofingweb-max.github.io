# Medical Facility Roofing

Industry served by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/industries/medical-roofing

Roofing for hospitals, clinics, and surgery centers in DFW and beyond.

- Infection control: containment, barriers, and timing set with the facilities team before the first core cut.
- Noise is sequenced away from patient-care areas and aligned with clinical schedules.
- Emergency access routes are mapped and protected before work begins, with staging and deliveries planned around them.
- Containment, access, work hours, rooftop equipment procedures, and documentation are agreed with facility leadership up front.
- Documentation supports warranty and regulatory needs.
- Example: The Good Shepherd in Longview, an acrylic coating restoration (see [Projects](../proof/projects.md)).
