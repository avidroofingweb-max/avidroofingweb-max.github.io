# Retail Roofing

Industry served by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/industries/retail-roofing

Roofing for stores, shopping centers, and multi-location retailers across DFW and 13 states.

- Work is planned around operating hours, customer traffic, deliveries, and peak sales periods, with protected entrances and safety perimeters set first.
- Inventory and sales-floor areas are protected first, and hidden intrusion is traced so damage doesn't surface weeks later.
- Repairs can be phased; multi-location rollouts are coordinated across stores.
- In an emergency, merchandise is protected and damage documented first, then the building is stabilized with temporary dry-in. Emergency line staffed 24/7; you will hear from AVID within 24 hours.
- Retail work includes Macy's and The Shops at La Cantera (see [Projects](../proof/projects.md)).
