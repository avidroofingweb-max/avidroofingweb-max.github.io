# Hotel Roofing

Industry served by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/industries/hotel-roofing

Roof repair and replacement for hotels and hospitality properties, scheduled around guest occupancy.

- Work is sequenced around house count, peak occupancy, and events, with noise and crew access kept away from occupied wings, entrances, and pool decks.
- Leak work targets seams, flashings, and penetrations, and finds the full extent of intrusion over guest rooms, not just the visible drip.
- Quiet scheduling, controlled debris, clear front-desk communication, and one point of accountability protect the guest experience.
- Systems (TPO, PVC, modified bitumen, coatings) are chosen after assessing the building, roof load, and rooftop equipment.
- Emergency line staffed 24/7; you will hear from AVID within 24 hours.
