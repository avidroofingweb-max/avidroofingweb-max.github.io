# Commercial Property Roofing

Industry served by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/industries/commercial-roofing

For apartment complexes, shopping centers, mixed-use properties, and portfolio owners and management companies, mainly in DFW.

## Concerns AVID addresses
Tenant disruption, water intrusion, capital forecasting, liability, premature replacement pushed by contractor incentives, warranty gaps from poor documentation, and one-size-fits-all treatment of a portfolio.

## How AVID works these properties
- A documented assessment of membrane, seams, flashings, penetrations, parapets, and drainage, with photos and moisture readings, separating immediate needs from deferrable ones.
- Repairs ranked by risk, remaining life, and budget into a phased capital plan.
- Tenant notices, staging, protected entrances, safety perimeters, noise control, and debris management planned before work starts.
- Multi-building work can be phased, with itemized bids and financing options presented openly.
- Common systems: TPO, PVC, modified bitumen, and metal, chosen for size, drainage, energy goals, and budget.
