# Restaurant Roofing

Industry served by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/industries/restaurant-roofing

Roofing for restaurants, with priority on the areas above kitchens, food prep, storage, and dining.

- Restaurant roofs fail more often because of many penetrations plus grease, heat, vibration, and foot traffic. Most failures start around exhaust fans, curbs, and penetrations.
- AVID inspects every exhaust fan, HVAC curb, pipe, drain, and penetration before scoping.
- PVC is often recommended for grease, oil, chemical, and heat resistance; the final choice depends on the existing roof, equipment, drainage, and grease exposure.
- Work is usually phased around service hours, with temporary dry-in keeping the building watertight.
- Emergency line staffed 24/7; you will hear from AVID within 24 hours.
