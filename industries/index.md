# AVID industries

Eight industry pages. Overview page: https://www.avidroofing.com/industries

- [Commercial Property Roofing](commercial-property.md) — Roofing for apartment complexes, shopping centers, mixed-use properties, and DFW portfolio owners.
- [Hotel Roofing](hotel.md) — Roof repair and replacement for hotels and hospitality properties, scheduled around occupancy and guests.
- [Industrial Roofing](industrial.md) — Roofing for warehouses, plants, and distribution centers, staged around production, shipping, and receiving.
- [Medical Facility Roofing](medical-facility.md) — Roofing for hospitals, clinics, and surgery centers with infection control, noise control, and protected emergency access.
- [Office Building Roofing](office-building.md) — Roofing for corporate offices, business parks, multi-tenant offices, and campuses, done while buildings stay occupied.
- [Restaurant Roofing](restaurant.md) — Restaurant roofing for grease, heat, and heavy rooftop equipment, phased around service hours.
- [Retail Roofing](retail.md) — Retail roofing that keeps stores open: inventory protection, phased work, and multi-location coordination.
- [Specialty Roofing](specialty.md) — Roofing for schools, municipal facilities, churches, historic properties, venues, and campuses that can't shut down.
