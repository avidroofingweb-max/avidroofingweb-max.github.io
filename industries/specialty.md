# Specialty Roofing

Industry served by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/industries/specialty-roofing

Roofing for schools, municipal facilities, public agencies, churches, historic properties, entertainment venues, and multi-building campuses: buildings whose occupancy can't be interrupted.

- Work is sequenced around school sessions, worship, performances, and public hours, with safety perimeters, protected entrances, and debris control.
- Historic work matches original profiles while delivering modern performance.
- Government and public work follows procurement and documentation rules.
- Phasing by building, section, funding period, or calendar; campuses get individual roof assessments under one coordinated plan.
- A dedicated project manager with daily updates and documentation through final warranty.
