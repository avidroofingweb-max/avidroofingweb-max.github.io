# Office Building Roofing

Industry served by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/industries/office-roofing

Roofing for corporate offices, professional buildings, business parks, mixed-use and multi-tenant offices, and campuses in DFW and nationwide.

- Most projects are completed with the building occupied, scheduled around business hours and tenant access, with disruptive work moved to low-occupancy periods.
- A reflective membrane with proper insulation can lower cooling load and HVAC cost.
- Failure prevention focuses on seams, drains, HVAC curbs, flashing, penetrations, and perimeter metal, and the existing roof stays protected until each section is watertight.
- Recommendations are based on roof age, moisture, insulation, drainage, membrane performance, and repair history, with immediate needs separated from deferrable work.
