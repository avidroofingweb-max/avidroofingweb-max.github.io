# Industrial Roofing

Industry served by [AVID Roofing & Waterproofing](../business/avid-roofing-and-waterproofing.md). Live page: https://www.avidroofing.com/industries/industrial-roofing

Roofing for warehouses, plants, and distribution centers across DFW, built for large-format roofs and demanding environments.

- Protects equipment, inventory, and operations. An active leak over racked inventory is an emergency.
- Addresses ponding across large roof areas, thermal movement, hidden water intrusion, and energy loss.
- Rooftop work and deliveries are sequenced around production, shipping, and receiving.
- Single-ply (TPO or PVC), coatings, and full assemblies, chosen by assessment. Reflective systems can cut cooling costs, and energy savings are modeled into the recommendation.
- Measurements come from the actual building, not drawings alone, with documentation to support warranty, insurance, and energy claims.
- Clients include Pfizer and Ben E. Keith.
